Platform

Seven AI engines, one unified platform

Each module strengthens the others through shared data and continuous learning.

◑

Terra Design AI

Generative building design that produces architectural layouts, structural drawings, foundation calculations, MEP, and bill of materials from a single prompt.

◈

Terra Materials AI

Optimizes compressed-earth block formulations using local soil, sand, lime, cement, fibers, and recycled materials to maximize strength while minimizing cost and carbon.

⚙

Terra Factory

Designs and controls automated production systems for blocks, panels, roofing, and structural components. Long-term: robotic micro-factories.

☀

Terra Energy

Optimizes orientation, window placement, thermal mass, ventilation, insulation, solar sizing, and battery storage to minimize lifetime energy costs.

⚙

Terra Construct

AI-assisted construction management coordinating materials, labor, equipment, scheduling, inspections, and safety. Future integration with autonomous machinery.

◆

Terra Twin

Every building gets a digital twin that monitors structural health, energy, moisture, cracks, maintenance needs, and occupancy trends for predictive maintenance.

◽

Terra City

Scales from buildings to neighborhoods and cities – optimizing roads, utilities, drainage, schools, hospitals, energy distribution, and public spaces.

Our Process

From consultation to smart living

We handle everything — from the first site survey to ongoing support.

01

Consultation & Site Survey

We visit your home, assess the electrical layout, network infrastructure, and your specific needs. We identify the best devices and technologies for your budget and lifestyle.

02

System Design & Proposal

We create a detailed system design with a clear, itemised quotation. You see every device, every service, and the total cost — no hidden fees, no surprises.

03

Installation & Configuration

Our team installs all devices, runs wiring where needed, sets up the Home Assistant server, configures Wi-Fi/Zigbee/Matter networks, and programs every automation.

04

Training & Ongoing Support

We train you and your family on how to use the system — voice commands, dashboards, schedules. Then we provide ongoing maintenance, updates, and expansion support.
