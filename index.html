Explore the Platform ↓ Our Vision
Imagine asking an AI: "Design a 500-home sustainable community in the UAE desert using local sand, powered by solar and battery storage, optimized for extreme heat and humidity, with a total construction cost below $20,000 per home."

Within minutes, TerraMind generates: structural designs, material recipes, foundation engineering, manufacturing instructions, factory schedules, energy systems, water management, carbon analysis, cost estimates, construction sequence, and maintenance predictions.
Platform

Seven AI engines, one unified platform

Each module strengthens the others through shared data and continuous learning.

◑

Terra Design AI

Generative building design that produces architectural layouts, structural drawings, foundation calculations, MEP, and bill of materials from a single prompt.

◈

Terra Materials AI

Optimizes compressed-earth block formulations using local soil, sand, lime, cement, fibers, and recycled materials to maximize strength while minimizing cost and carbon.

⚙

Terra Factory

Designs and controls automated production systems for blocks, panels, roofing, and structural components. Long-term: robotic micro-factories.

☀

Terra Energy

Optimizes orientation, window placement, thermal mass, ventilation, insulation, solar sizing, and battery storage to minimize lifetime energy costs.

⚙

Terra Construct

AI-assisted construction management coordinating materials, labor, equipment, scheduling, inspections, and safety. Future integration with autonomous machinery.

◆

Terra Twin

Every building gets a digital twin that monitors structural health, energy, moisture, cracks, maintenance needs, and occupancy trends for predictive maintenance.

◽

Terra City

Scales from buildings to neighborhoods and cities — optimizing roads, utilities, drainage, schools, hospitals, energy distribution, and public spaces.

How It Works

From problem to platform

Start with one indispensable capability, then expand into a full platform.

01

AI Material & Building Optimizer (MVP)

Upload soil characteristics, site location, building type, and budget. TerraMind returns an optimized earth mix, wall and foundation specs, manufacturing parameters, cost and carbon estimates, and energy recommendations.

02

Generative Building Design

AI generates complete building designs optimized for cost, climate, local materials, carbon, and energy — simultaneously, not sequentially.

03

Manufacturing & Construction Automation

Automated production lines, AI-assisted construction management, and semi-autonomous micro-factories for local manufacturing.

04

Digital Twins & Smart Infrastructure

Every building gets a digital brain. Continuous monitoring, predictive maintenance, and city-scale optimization.
